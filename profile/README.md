# CML Platform

Open-source software and data from the [Institute of Environmental Sciences (CML)](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/environmental-sciences) at [Leiden University](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en).

## About CML

CML addresses sustainability challenges at local, national, and global scale.
Our research looks at pollution, resource depletion, climate change, urbanisation, and biodiversity loss.
These challenges are connected, so we study them with integrated, system-based approaches.

CML has two research departments:

- **Industrial Ecology**
- **Environmental Biology**

Five research programmes connect the two departments:

- [Biodiversity](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/environmental-sciences/research/biodiversity)
- [Food](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/environmental-sciences/research/food-research)
- [Resources and Energy](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/environmental-sciences/research/resources-and-energy)
- [Urban](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/environmental-sciences/research/urban)
- [Water](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/environmental-sciences/research/water)

## About this organization

This GitHub organization holds research software, models, and datasets made by CML researchers.
